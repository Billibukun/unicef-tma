How to scan a device

Point your camera at the barcodes on the back of the device (e.g., OUKITEL RT7 TITAN). Each barcode encodes an IMEI number. The Serial Number (S/N) is usually printed as text only — enter it manually below. Scan the first barcode for IMEI 1, the second for IMEI 2.
